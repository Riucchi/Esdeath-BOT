Welcome to EsdeathBOT Commands 💗

Our bot provides the service to play your favorite music through YouTube URLs in your Discord channel!

Commands Invite it to your server





EsdeathBOT Commands
These are the available commands:
/join

With this command, you can bring the bot into the Discord channel you are in.

/play + url

This is the command you will use to play any YouTube URL, except for certain types of videos such as those exceeding 30 minutes in duration, for example.

/pause

This command is used to pause the song you are currently playing.

/resume

You will use this command when you need to play again the song that was paused with the "/pause" command.

/skip

This is the command you will use when you want to skip to the next song in your custom playlist, or one that you have sent via a URL.

/stop

This command will stop the music playback, as well as remove the bot from the channel it is in.
